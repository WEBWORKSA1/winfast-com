Questions

Is WinFast.com free?

Yes. Browsing, tools, guides and Win Alerts are free. We're supported by advertising, sponsors and voluntary donations.

Do you run the sweepstakes you list?

No. Listings link to official sponsor pages. Always read the sponsor's official rules. We only run our own WinFast contests, under our published official rules.

How is the directory kept up to date?

A scheduled job refreshes the live hackathon feed every day and automatically hides anything past its deadline. Curated listings are reviewed regularly, and readers can submit new opportunities.

Do you list gambling or lottery sites?

No. We don't list betting, casino, 'sweepstakes casino' or lottery-ticket products.

I'm a brand — how do I get my giveaway featured?

Submit it free, or choose a paid featured placement or done-for-you campaign on our For Brands page.
