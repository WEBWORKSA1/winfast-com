The Fast-Win Playbook

21 practical moves to win more — the same playbook we send to Win Alerts subscribers.

Part 1 — Choose better odds

  1. Prefer many prizes over one huge prize. Ten $100 prizes often beat one $5,000 prize in expected value.
  2. Go local. Regional promotions get a fraction of national entries.
  3. Go effortful. Photo, essay and video contests scare off casual entrants.
  4. Check eligibility edges. State-, province- or age-limited promotions shrink the pool.
  5. Use the mail-in route when it's offered — it's often the least used.
  6. Run the numbers with the odds calculator.

Part 2 — Enter like a system

  1. Make a 15-minute daily routine.
  2. Use one dedicated email and check spam daily.
  3. Save opportunities with ☆ and import them into the entry tracker.
  4. Sort by deadline — expiring first.
  5. Never break entry limits — it can void all your entries.
  6. Log every win and its value for tax time.

Part 3 — Win judged competitions

  1. Treat the brief as the scoring sheet.
  2. Study past winners.
  3. Demo first in hackathons — full playbook.
  4. Opt into every sponsor prize you qualify for.
  5. Submit 48 hours early.

Part 4 — Protect your wins

  1. Never pay to claim a prize. Spot scams.
  2. Verify winner messages on the sponsor's official site.
  3. Estimate taxes before accepting big non-cash prizes — estimator.
  4. Read rights clauses in creative contests.
