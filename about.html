About WinFast.com

We help people find legitimate opportunities to win — faster — and help organisers run fair, compliant promotions.

Our mission

Opportunities to win cash, scholarships, funding, gear and recognition are everywhere — scattered across thousands of sites, buried in rules, and mixed with scams. WinFast.com brings the legitimate ones into one fast, searchable place and gives everyone free tools to enter smarter and run fairer draws.

What we do

  • Curate free-to-enter sweepstakes, hackathons, prize challenges, scholarships and creative competitions, always linking to official pages.
  • Automate freshness: a daily job refreshes our live feed and removes expired listings.
  • Educate with original guides on strategy, safety, taxes and compliance.
  • Equip with free tools — winner picker, wheel, odds and tax calculators, tracker and rules generator.

What we don't do

We don't run third-party promotions, sell entries, or list gambling, betting, casino-style or lottery-ticket products. We never ask winners for money.

How we make money

Advertising (Google AdSense), clearly labelled featured listings and sponsorships, done-for-you campaign services for brands, and voluntary donations. Paid placement never changes our editorial guides. See our editorial policy.

Independence

WinFast.com is an independent website. The name is a descriptive domain (“win fast”) and is not affiliated with any company that uses “WinFast” as a trademark. See our disclaimer & trademark notice.
