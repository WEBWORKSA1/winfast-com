FAQ

How are the odds calculated?

For a random draw of W prizes from N total entries where you hold K entries (one prize per entry drawn without replacement), your chance of winning nothing is the product of (N−K−i)/(N−i) for i from 0 to W−1. We show one minus that.

How do I estimate total entries?

Some rules disclose entry counts after past draws; social giveaways show comment counts. When unknown, compare a pessimistic and an optimistic estimate.

Do more entries really help?

Yes — linearly for small odds. Doubling your valid entries roughly doubles your chance in large promotions.
