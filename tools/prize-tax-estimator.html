FAQ

Do I pay tax on a sweepstakes prize in the US?

Generally yes. The IRS treats prizes as taxable income at fair market value, whether cash or non-cash, and they're reported on your federal return.

Are prizes taxable in Canada?

Prizes won by chance as an individual are generally not taxable in Canada, but prizes connected to employment or business can be, and investment income earned on winnings is taxable.

Can I refuse a prize because of the tax?

Usually yes. Declining a prize generally means you don't receive it and don't owe tax on it — check the official rules and a tax professional.
