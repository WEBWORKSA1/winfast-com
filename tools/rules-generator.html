FAQ

Is this a legal document?

It's a structured template covering the sections most official rules include. It isn't legal advice — have counsel review it for your jurisdictions.

Why does a sweepstakes need 'no purchase necessary'?

Combining a prize, chance and a required payment generally creates an illegal lottery. A free method of entry removes the payment element.

What's different for Canada?

Canadian promotions typically require winners to answer a skill-testing question, and Quebec has extra requirements.
