Editorial Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Sources

Listings link to official pages. Guides are written from primary sources (official rules, government and tax-authority guidance, platform policies) and our own testing of tools.

Verification

We check that each listing has an official page and published rules, that sweepstakes have a free entry method, and that fees are disclosed. Live feed items come from organisers' public listings and are removed automatically after their deadline.

Independence

Advertisers and sponsors cannot buy editorial coverage in guides. Paid listings are labelled “Featured” or “Sponsored”.

Corrections

Found an error? Tell us (topic: Listing correction). We correct promptly and update the page date.
