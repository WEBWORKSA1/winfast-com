Official Contest Rules

Last updated: November 17, 2026

NO PURCHASE OR PAYMENT NECESSARY TO ENTER OR WIN. VOID WHERE PROHIBITED. These rules apply to skill contests run by WinFast.com (“Organiser”) and announced on the Contests page. Each round's theme, prize, dates and judging criteria are published on that page and form part of these rules.

1. Eligibility

Open to individuals who are at least the age of majority in their place of residence (or 13+ with parental consent where a round explicitly allows it), except where prohibited by law. Organiser's staff and their households are not eligible. Residents of jurisdictions where the contest is unlawful are excluded.

2. How to enter

Submit the entry form on the Contests page during the entry period. One entry per person per round. Entries must be original, not previously awarded, and must not infringe third-party rights or contain unlawful or offensive content.

3. Judging

This is a contest of skill. Entries are scored by judges appointed by the Organiser using the published criteria with equal weighting unless stated otherwise. Ties are broken by the highest score in the first criterion. Judges' decisions are final.

4. Prizes

Prizes are as stated for each round. Prizes may be funded by sponsors; sponsor names will be disclosed. No cash substitution except at Organiser's discretion. Winners are responsible for applicable taxes.

5. Canadian residents

Canadian residents selected as potential winners must correctly answer a time-limited mathematical skill-testing question before being confirmed. Quebec-specific requirements will be stated where applicable.

6. Notification

Potential winners are contacted by email and must respond within 7 days and may be asked to confirm eligibility. Otherwise an alternate may be selected.

7. Licence

Entrants keep ownership of their entries and grant the Organiser a non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to display the entry and the entrant's name in connection with the contest.

8. Privacy

Personal information is used to administer the contest as described in our Privacy Policy.

9. General

The Organiser may cancel or modify a round if fraud, technical issues or other factors impair its integrity. This contest is not sponsored, endorsed or administered by any social media platform.
