Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This policy explains what information WinFast.com (“we”, “us”) collects and how we use it.

Information you give us

When you submit a form (Win Alerts, contact, quote, application, contest entry, submission or pledge), we receive the information you enter, such as your name, email and message. Forms are delivered to our private inbox through a third-party form-delivery service (FormSubmit). We use this information only to respond, send the emails you asked for, administer contests and evaluate applications.

Information stored on your device

Our tools (tracker, saved opportunities, deadlines, theme) store data in your browser's local storage. This data stays on your device and is not sent to us.

Advertising and cookies

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to our site and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies at aboutads.info. Learn more about how Google uses data from partner sites.

Embedded content

Videos are embedded from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode and load only when you press play. Fonts are served by Google Fonts. Donations are processed by PayPal on its own site under its privacy policy.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. We share it only with service providers needed to operate the site, or when required by law.

Retention

We keep form submissions only as long as needed for the purpose they were submitted for, then delete them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g. GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, PIPEDA, Quebec Law 25) you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to processing of your data, and to withdraw consent. Use our contact form (topic: Privacy request). You can unsubscribe from emails at any time.

Children

WinFast.com is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. Many listed opportunities are restricted to adults.

Changes

We may update this policy and will revise the date above.
