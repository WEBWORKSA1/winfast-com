How to Run a Legal, High-Converting Giveaway (For Brands & Creators)

A practical checklist for running a giveaway that grows your list and stays compliant: rules, eligibility, platforms, fair draws and follow-up.

By the WinFast.com Editorial Team · Reviewed against official sources · Editorial policy

A well-run giveaway can add thousands of subscribers in a week. A badly-run one can break platform rules, attract fraud, or create legal exposure. Here's the checklist we use.

1. Decide the format

  • Sweepstakes (random draw) — easiest to scale, needs a free entry route.
  • Contest (judged) — great for user-generated content, needs clear criteria and judges.
  • Instant win — exciting, but needs pre-seeded winning moments and careful odds disclosure.

2. Write official rules

At minimum: sponsor name and address, eligibility (age, location), start and end date/time with time zone, how to enter and entry limits, prize description and ARV, number of winners, odds statement, how and when winners are selected and notified, and a privacy notice. Start with our free official rules generator, then have counsel review it.

3. Respect platform rules

  • Instagram and Facebook require that you state the promotion is not sponsored, endorsed or administered by Meta, and they don't allow tagging people in content they're not in as an entry action.
  • YouTube requires you to publish rules and handle the promotion yourself — YouTube is not a sponsor.
  • Don't require shares for entry where a platform disallows it.

4. Maximise conversion — ethically

  • One clear prize your ideal customer wants beats a generic gadget.
  • Primary entry: email. Bonus entries: referrals, follows, quiz answers.
  • Mobile-first landing page, one form field above the fold.
  • Send a reminder 24 hours before closing.

5. Pick winners fairly — and prove it

Remove duplicates and ineligible entries, then draw with a cryptographically random tool like our giveaway winner picker. Record the draw (screen recording or certificate) and keep alternates.

6. Follow up

Announce winners as your rules promise, deliver prizes promptly, and send all entrants a thank-you with a consolation offer. That email is where giveaway ROI is made.
