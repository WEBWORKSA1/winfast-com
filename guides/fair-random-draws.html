How to Run a Provably Fair Random Draw

Why Math.random isn't enough, how cryptographic randomness works, and how to document a giveaway draw people trust.

By the WinFast.com Editorial Team · Reviewed against official sources · Editorial policy

Accusations of rigged giveaways can damage a brand in hours. A fair draw is easy — if you do it transparently.

1. Clean the entry list first

  • Remove duplicates according to your rules (one per person or one per day).
  • Remove ineligible entries (location, age, employees).
  • Freeze the final list and save a copy with a timestamp.

2. Use cryptographic randomness

General-purpose random functions in many programming languages are designed for speed, not unpredictability. Modern browsers provide crypto.getRandomValues(), a cryptographically secure generator. Our giveaway picker uses it with rejection sampling so every entry has exactly equal probability.

3. Document the draw

  • Screen-record the draw or livestream it.
  • Publish the number of eligible entries and the draw time.
  • Draw alternates at the same time in case a winner is ineligible or unresponsive.

4. Notify and verify

Contact winners exactly as your rules state, give a fair response window, and verify eligibility before announcing publicly.
