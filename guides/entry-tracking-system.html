Build an Entry-Tracking System That Saves Hours

How serious entrants organise daily entries, deadlines, winner notifications and taxes with a simple tracking system.

By the WinFast.com Editorial Team · Reviewed against official sources · Editorial policy

Organisation is the difference between casual luck and consistent wins. A tracking system makes sure you never miss a daily entry, a deadline, or a winner email.

FieldWhy

Daily workflow

  1. Sort by end date; enter anything ending within 7 days first.
  2. Enter all daily promotions.
  3. Log wins immediately with value and sponsor — you'll need it for taxes.
